Persistent NPCs & token templates

Moving a named NPC keeps the same identity and removes its old placement. Templates create independent tokens with new private links.

Atmosphere

Sound plays from the table window after its fullscreen click. Effects appear only over revealed map areas. Blanking mutes sound and hides effects. Your preview stays silent.

Compass overlay

0° points north toward the top of the original map. The compass follows table rotation and stays in the upper-right corner. Adjust it even while the map is locked. Saved per map.

Table position, scale & rotation

Physical calibration

Use two adjacent grid intersections horizontally or vertically to measure map pixels. For a gridless map, set pixels and grid origin manually. Measure a revealed square on the monitor. New PPI = current PPI × desired inches ÷ measured inches. Reapply and lock before placing minis.
